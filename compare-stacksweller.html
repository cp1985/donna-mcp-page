Donna vs StackSweller

StackSweller is a Substack-only cloud queue: unlimited notes, AI that turns posts into Notes, one price. Donna is the decision layer before anything hits a queue.

What StackSweller actually is

StackSweller sells one Pro plan: $39/month or $25/month billed annually ($250/year). Unlimited scheduled Notes and unlimited AI credits. It is Substack-only, cloud-based, so the laptop can be off. AI turns a long post into 7+ Notes. There is a free Chrome extension for lighter scheduling that does require the browser to be open. Setup for the cloud product has involved a stored Substack session that expires and needs reconnecting. The product is still described as beta in some writeups. No cross-posting.

What Donna actually is

Donna does not try to be the cheapest unlimited queue. It loads your Creator Portrait into Claude, proposes the week, and schedules what you approve. $30/month.

Who should pick StackSweller

You want one flat price, unlimited Notes, and a cloud queue. You already know what to write. The reconnect ritual is acceptable.

Who should pick Donna

A queue is not the bottleneck. Deciding the week without re-briefing Claude is. Donna is that, not an unlimited Notes mill.
