Donna vs StackSweller

StackSweller is a Substack-only cloud queue: unlimited notes, AI that turns posts into Notes, one price. Donna is the decision layer before anything hits a queue.

Facts checked against each tool's own site on 25 October 2026. Prices change; check before you buy.

What StackSweller actually is

StackSweller sells one Pro plan: $25 a month, or $250 a year (which it says saves $50), with a 7-day free trial. Unlimited scheduled Notes and unlimited AI credits. It is Substack-only, cloud-based, so the laptop can be off. AI turns a long post into 7+ Notes. There is a free Chrome extension for lighter scheduling. The browser must be open when you schedule and the computer on at the scheduled time. StackSweller’s own FAQ says Substack sessions expire from time to time, and you reconnect through the extension or a fresh cookie. The site says it has a few bugs here and there. Cross-posting is not mentioned.

What Donna actually is

Donna does not try to be the cheapest unlimited queue. It loads your Creator Portrait into Claude, proposes the week, and schedules the Notes you approve. $30/month.

Who should pick StackSweller

You want one flat price, unlimited Notes, and a cloud queue. You already know what to write. Reconnecting your Substack session now and then is acceptable.

Who should pick Donna

A queue is not the bottleneck. Deciding the week without re-briefing Claude is. Donna is that, not an unlimited Notes mill.
