Donna vs WriteStack

WriteStack is the deepest Substack-only growth suite. Donna is a 2nd brain that closes the weekly decision in Claude. They overlap on scheduling. They are not the same product.

Facts checked against each tool's own site on 25 October 2026. Prices change; check before you buy.

What WriteStack actually is

WriteStack is a Substack-only suite built around Notes. You queue Notes, schedule them in WriteStack, and click Sync so a browser extension publishes them to Substack. You also get analytics, AI drafting (Quill) that learns from your published work, and MCP tools to draft, tag, check capacity, and queue. Plans are $23.99, $32.99, and $99.99 a month (Hobbyist, Standard, Enterprise), with annual billing from $19.99 a month. Every plan has a 7-day trial. There is no free plan. Notes only: WriteStack says it is not meant for publishing long-form Substack posts.

Its real strength is the layer Substack itself does not give you: analytics on which Notes brought free and paid subscribers, and a queue built for Notes. Substack added native Notes scheduling in 2026. What WriteStack sells on top of that is the queue and the data, not the act of scheduling one Note.

What Donna actually is

Donna is a Claude MCP that loads a Creator Portrait from your Notion — voice, audience, strategy, past decisions, what’s already live — then proposes what to write, what’s working, and what’s next. You approve. The Chrome extension puts your approved Notes into your Substack drafts with the times you set. Nothing publishes in the sitting. Your portrait stays in your Notion. $30/month after a 7-day trial.

Who should pick WriteStack

You already know what to write. The pain is the queue and the analytics. You want a Substack-only dashboard and do not need a second brain that remembers last week’s strategy. WriteStack is the better tool for that job, and it is often cheaper.

Who should pick Donna

You already make money on Substack. The thing eating the week is deciding what to write, reading what’s working, and choosing what’s next — then briefing Claude again because the last chat forgot you. Donna loads that context from Notion and closes the week in one sitting. She is not a replacement for WriteStack’s analytics dashboard.
