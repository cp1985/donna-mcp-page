Donna vs Narrareach

Narrareach is a distribution engine across Substack, Medium, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, and Threads. Donna is Substack-only and lives inside Claude. Breadth versus depth.

What Narrareach actually is

Narrareach is a cloud publishing desk. You write or import an article, AI cuts it into Notes and social posts, you batch-schedule, and you track which posts appear to drive subscribers. Paid plans start at $19/month (Audience Builder), then $39 and $89, with caps on editorial cycles, AI actions, and connected publications. Every plan schedules Substack Notes and articles. Higher tiers add more platforms, paid-post handling, team seats, API, and MCP for Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. A free plan and a 7-day trial exist.

Publishing to Substack depends on a session. Their own docs and third-party writeups note that the queue relies on a healthy Substack session that periodically needs reconnecting. The product is built for people who publish beyond Substack every week.

What Donna actually is

Donna is Claude-native. It loads your Creator Portrait from Notion and closes what to write, what’s working, and what’s next in one sitting. The extension schedules approved Substack pieces. It does not cross-post to LinkedIn or Medium. $30/month.

Who should pick Narrareach

You genuinely publish on Medium, LinkedIn, or X as well as Substack. The job is distribution and repurposing, not only deciding the Substack week. Narrareach is the better tool for that.

Who should pick Donna

Substack is the home. Cross-posting is occasional. The pain is still the three questions every week, answered in a blank Claude chat. Donna is narrower on purpose.
