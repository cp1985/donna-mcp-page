Donna vs Narrareach

Narrareach is a distribution engine across Substack, Medium, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, and Threads. Donna is Substack-only and lives inside Claude. Breadth versus depth.

Facts checked against each tool's own site on 25 October 2026. Prices change; check before you buy.

What Narrareach actually is

Narrareach is a cloud publishing desk. You write or import an article, AI cuts it into Notes and social posts, you batch-schedule, and you track which posts appear to drive subscribers. Plans are $19.99 (Audience Builder), $39 (Revenue Writer), and $89 (Full Agentic Mode) a month, with caps on monthly cycles, daily AI actions, and connected Substack publications. Every plan schedules Substack Notes and articles. Revenue Writer adds more platforms and paid Substack posts. Full Agentic Mode adds a REST API and webhooks. An official MCP is included from the first plan up. There are no team seats, only guest collaborators on each draft. There is no free plan. Every plan has a 7-day trial with no card, and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Beyond the six networks above, it also covers Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, WordPress, and Ghost. The product is built for people who publish beyond Substack every week.

What Donna actually is

Donna is Claude-native. It loads your Creator Portrait from Notion and closes what to write, what’s working, and what’s next in one sitting. The extension puts your approved Notes into your Substack drafts with the times you set. It does not cross-post to LinkedIn or Medium. $30/month.

Who should pick Narrareach

You genuinely publish on Medium, LinkedIn, or X as well as Substack. The job is distribution and repurposing, not only deciding the Substack week. Narrareach is the better tool for that.

Who should pick Donna

Substack is the home. Cross-posting is occasional. The pain is still the three questions every week, answered in a blank Claude chat. Donna is narrower on purpose.
