How to schedule Substack Notes

How to schedule Substack Notes in 2026

Native Substack schedules one Note at a time. Once you post daily, the real job is deciding the week and batching only what you approve.

Native

What Substack’s built-in scheduler does

On web, open the Notes composer, click the calendar icon, pick a time, and schedule. On mobile, use the three-dot menu. Notes can be queued ahead and publish from Substack’s servers.

That is enough if you post a few Notes a week. It is one item at a time, with no queue view for a full week and no help deciding what belongs on the calendar.

Batch

When you need more than one-at-a-time

Daily Notes create a different problem: you still have to decide what to write, keep voice consistent, and get a week onto the calendar without rebuilding context every session.

Donna loads your Creator Portrait from Notion inside Claude, proposes the week, drafts the pieces you approve, and the extension schedules them. Nothing publishes until you say so.
