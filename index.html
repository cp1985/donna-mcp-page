One sitting in Claude, three steps

A sitting is one session with Donna in Claude.

  1. Open Claude. Your context is already there.

    Donna loads your Creator Portrait from your Notion: voice, audience, strategy and past decisions. You do not re-explain your niche or paste in old posts.

  2. Donna proposes next week. You decide.

    She suggests what to write, says what is working from what she has read of your Substack, and drafts in your voice. Nothing moves until you approve it.

  3. Your approved Notes get their times.

    You approve the Notes and set the times in Claude. Donna saves them. One click in the Chrome extension puts them into your Substack drafts. Essays you publish yourself.

How scheduling works

Approve your Notes in Claude; the Chrome extension puts them into your Substack drafts with the times you set. Substack sends them at that time; you can edit or delete any.

Scheduling is for Notes, not essays. Essays are drafted with you and you publish them yourself. Donna does not log in to Substack for you.

Questions before you start

I'm not technical.

Setup is: paste a link into Claude, connect Notion, follow the guide. If you can send a Substack email, you can do it.

Do I need to pay for Notion?

No. Notion's free plan is fine.

Do I need a paid Claude subscription?

No, but recommended because usage limits on the free plan can cut a long sitting short. Never tested on ChatGPT.

Will it sound like me?

Donna builds a voice reference from your published work. It will not be perfect on day one. It gets better as your portrait fills in.

Is my Substack password stored?

No.

What do you store?

Your email, your credit balance and usage. Notes and drafts your extension syncs are kept for 24 hours. Notes you approve for scheduling are kept until your extension pushes them, or 60 days at most. Your Creator Portrait, plans and decisions live in your own Notion, not on our servers.

How do credits work?

You get 250 credits when you start and 250 more each month. An audit or a full essay costs 3. A Note, hook, plan or strategy check costs 1. Getting started, logging a decision, reading your Substack sync and scheduling Notes are free.

What if I cancel?

Cancel anytime from Gumroad. Access and credits end. Everything in your Notion stays yours.

What Donna is not

  • Donna does not choose what to post. She proposes and drafts. You decide.
  • Donna schedules Notes only. The extension puts approved Notes into your Substack drafts with the times you set, and Substack sends them. Essays are not scheduled, and Donna does not post for you.
  • Donna does not open your Substack analytics dashboard. The extension syncs your drafts and published Notes.
  • Donna does not replace your judgment. She takes the weekly decisions off your plate.
  • Donna does not write without context. The one-time audit is required first.
