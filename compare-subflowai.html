Donna vs SubflowAI

SubflowAI is a Chrome extension for batch-scheduling Notes, AI variations, and a visual calendar. Donna is a Claude-native 2nd brain. Overlap is scheduling and MCP. The jobs are different.

What SubflowAI actually is

SubflowAI (genaiunplugged.com) is a Chrome extension that schedules Substack Notes in batches, generates AI variations with a Brand Voice, and shows a visual calendar. Pro is about $9.97/month. A 5-year plan is $69.97. Premium is $99.97 and includes Connect to Claude and ChatGPT (MCP); on other plans MCP is a $30 add-on. It claims server-side handoff so Notes can publish with Chrome closed, plus analytics and remix of top notes. Your Substack password is not stored.

It is the cheapest serious batch scheduler in this set. The product is the queue and the AI variations, not a strategy system that remembers last week’s decisions.

What Donna actually is

Donna loads a Creator Portrait from your Notion into Claude, proposes the week, drafts in your lane, and schedules only what you approve. $30/month after a 7-day trial. Context stays in your Notion.

Who should pick Donna

The bottleneck is not the queue. It is deciding what belongs on the calendar this week, in your voice, without re-briefing Claude. Donna is that sitting. She is not the cheapest scheduler.
