Donna vs SubflowAI

SubflowAI is a Chrome extension for batch-scheduling Notes, AI variations, and a visual calendar. Donna is a Claude-native 2nd brain. Overlap is scheduling and MCP. The jobs are different.

Facts checked against each tool's own site on 25 October 2026. Prices change; check before you buy.

What SubflowAI actually is

SubflowAI (genaiunplugged.com) is a Chrome extension that schedules Substack Notes in batches, generates AI variations with a Brand Voice, and shows a visual calendar. Pro is $9.97 a month. A 5-year plan is $69.97. Premium is $99.97 and is the 5-year plan plus Connect to Claude and ChatGPT, for life. On the monthly and 5-year plans, Connect is a one-time $30 add-on. There is a 5-day free trial with no card, and a 14-day refund. Scheduled Notes sit on Substack’s servers, so they publish with Chrome closed. It also has analytics and a remix of top Notes. It says your Substack password is never stored or transmitted, and it works through your browser session.

It is the cheapest paid monthly batch scheduler in this set. The product is the queue and the AI variations, not a strategy system that remembers last week’s decisions.

What Donna actually is

Donna loads a Creator Portrait from your Notion into Claude, proposes the week, drafts in your lane, and schedules only the Notes you approve. $30/month after a 7-day trial. Your portrait stays in your Notion.

Who should pick SubflowAI

You know what to post. You want to batch 30 Notes, remix what worked, and spend as little as possible. The $9.97 plan is hard to beat on pure scheduling price.

Who should pick Donna

The bottleneck is not the queue. It is deciding what belongs on the calendar this week, in your voice, without re-briefing Claude. Donna is that sitting. She is not the cheapest scheduler.
