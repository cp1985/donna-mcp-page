Why people look for a WriteStack alternative

WriteStack does the Substack-only queue well. After Substack added native Notes scheduling, people still pay for it because of the analytics, the AI drafting, and the ability to batch a week. If you are thinking of leaving, it is probably not because the queue failed. It is because you still open Claude every Monday, re-explain the niche, and get generic drafts.

What you keep if you stay on WriteStack

Analytics on which Notes bring free and paid subscribers. AI drafting that learns from your published work. MCP tools to draft, tag, check capacity, and queue. A browser extension that syncs your scheduled Notes to Substack. That stack is real, and Donna does not replace the analytics dashboard.

What changes if you switch to Donna

You sit in Claude. Donna loads a Creator Portrait from your Notion — voice, audience, strategy, past decisions, what’s already live. She proposes the week. You approve. The extension puts the Notes you approved into your Substack drafts with the times you set. Nothing publishes until that scheduled time. Your portrait stays in your Notion. $30/month after a 7-day trial.

When Donna is the right alternative

You already make money on Substack. You know the queue works. The week still dies on “what to write, what’s working, what’s next.” You want that closed in one sitting, with the portrait you own.

When WriteStack is still the better buy

You want the data layer and the AI drafting. You already know what to post. You want the cheaper Substack-only suite. Stay.
