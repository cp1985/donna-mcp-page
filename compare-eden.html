Donna vs Eden

Eden is a full creator workspace: research, scheduling across eight platforms, and native handoff of Substack Notes and articles. Donna is a Substack sitting inside Claude.

Facts checked against each tool's own site on 25 October 2026. Prices change; check before you buy.

What Eden actually is

Eden schedules to X, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, and Substack. For Substack, text Notes and articles go to Substack’s own scheduler and post with your devices off. Image Notes are handed off through Chrome with the extension. Threads of Notes post live, so Chrome with the extension or the Mac app must be on within 10 minutes of the slot. Eden’s own pages are not fully consistent on image Notes. Social scheduling starts on the Starter plan. Eden’s own pages give two Starter prices: $25 a month billed yearly ($299 a year), and $29 a month billed monthly. The Free and Personal plans do not include social scheduling. Eden also includes Discover (research and outlier search), Custom AI on Pro and above, analytics history of 30, 90, or 365 days depending on the plan, and an MCP that can draft, schedule, publish, and pull analytics.

It is one of the broadest products in this set. It is also the one most people will not need if Substack is the only channel that pays them.

What Donna actually is

Donna does not schedule Instagram or X. It loads your Substack strategy from Notion into Claude, proposes the week, and schedules the Notes you approve. $30/month.
