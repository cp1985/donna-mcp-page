Donna vs Eden

Eden is a full creator workspace: research, scheduling across eight platforms, and native handoff of Substack Notes and articles. Donna is a Substack sitting inside Claude.

What Eden actually is

Eden schedules to X, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, and Substack. For Substack, most text Notes and articles are handed to Substack’s own scheduler, so they can publish with devices off once the handoff is done. Threads and some image Notes still need Chrome or the desktop app near the slot. Scheduling is on Starter and up, from about $29/month (or ~$25 billed yearly). There is a free plan and a $5 Discover plan with a tiny scheduling cap. Eden also includes a research feed, Custom AI, analytics history on paid plans, and MCP for social posts and scheduling.

It is the broadest product in this set. It is also the one most people will not need if Substack is the only channel that pays them.

What Donna actually is

Donna does not schedule Instagram or X. It loads your Substack strategy from Notion into Claude, proposes the week, and schedules what you approve. $30/month.
