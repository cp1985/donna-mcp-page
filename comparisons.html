Donna vs the Substack tools people actually use

These are not “we win every row” pages. Each comparison says what the other tool does well, where it is the better buy, and where Donna is a different job.

Donna vs WriteStack

Substack-only growth suite: queue, viral-notes research, subscriber analytics, MCP on higher tiers. Strong if the job is the queue and the data.

Donna vs SubflowAI

Chrome extension for batch Notes, AI variations, visual calendar. MCP on the premium plan. Cheapest serious scheduler in the set.

Donna vs Narrareach

Multi-platform publishing desk. Article-to-Notes, cross-post, MCP. Better if you publish beyond Substack every week.

Donna vs Eden

Cross-platform system with native Substack handoff for Notes and articles, plus research. Broader than a Substack-only tool.

Donna vs StackSweller

Simple cloud Notes queue at one flat price. Unlimited notes and AI credits. Substack only.

Donna vs StackBuddy

Low-priced scheduler with a real free plan and a built-in creator network. Hands Notes to Substack’s native scheduler.
