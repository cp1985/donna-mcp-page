Donna vs StackBuddy

StackBuddy is a focused scheduler with a real free plan, a creator network, and native handoff into Substack. Donna is the sitting that decides what goes on that calendar.

Facts checked against each tool's own site on 25 October 2026. Prices change; check before you buy.

What StackBuddy actually is

StackBuddy is a web app plus Chrome extension. You write Notes in the app; the extension pushes them into Substack’s native scheduler, so Substack’s servers publish with Chrome closed. Free plan: 30 scheduled Notes a month, full creator network access, 10 AI credits a month. Pro is $12.50/month billed yearly ($150/year) or $15 month-to-month: unlimited scheduling, 300 AI credits a month, AI thumbnails, LinkedIn cross-posting, full analytics. A 14-day Pro trial needs no card. Its standout feature is a built-in creator network, which looks aimed at finding other creators to work with.

What Donna actually is

Donna does not have a free forever plan or a creator network. It loads your portrait into Claude and closes the week, and your approved Notes are scheduled. $30/month after the 7-day trial.
