Donna vs StackBuddy

StackBuddy is a focused scheduler with a real free plan, a creator network, and native handoff into Substack. Donna is the sitting that decides what goes on that calendar.

What StackBuddy actually is

StackBuddy is a web app plus Chrome extension. You write Notes in the app; the extension pushes them into Substack’s native scheduler, so Substack’s servers publish with Chrome closed. Free plan: 30 scheduled Notes a month, the creator network, 10 AI credits. Pro is $12.50/month billed yearly ($150/year) or $15 month-to-month: unlimited scheduling, 300 AI credits, AI images, LinkedIn cross-post, analytics. A 14-day Pro trial needs no card. The standout feature none of the others in this set match is a built-in creator network for finding people to restack and cross-promote with. User base is still small.

What Donna actually is

Donna does not have a free forever plan or a creator network. It loads your portrait into Claude and closes the week. $30/month after trial.
